Frequently Asked Questions

Clarifying Our Advisory Model

When is the ideal time to engage Pyramid / EnvaDure?

The most cost-effective stage is during Pre-Tender (when architectural and structural schemes are being frozen). Incorporating fit-for-purpose performance clauses and customized CAD cross-sections into the tender document eliminates change orders, price escalations, and contractor disputes.

Do you sell waterproofing chemicals or represent specific manufacturers?

Absolutely not. We operate on radical brand neutrality. We do not sell chemicals, hold dealerships, or accept vendor commissions. Our allegiance is solely to the structural performance of your building.

How do you protect drawing confidentiality?

We routinely execute bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) with developers, architects, and corporate asset owners before reviewing drawing sets or geotechnical reports.
