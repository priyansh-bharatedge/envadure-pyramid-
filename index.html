Why are we designing modern structures using 20th-century specifications? Data from the Deloitte & Rainmatter State of Climate Response report reveals extreme stresses on building envelopes:

Increase in Extreme Heatwave Days

India experienced a 15-fold surge in heatwave days across Mar–Sept over the last 3 decades, creating severe diurnal thermal stress.

1.4x

Rise in Extreme Rainfall Events

1.4x rise in torrential cloudburst events across Indian metro hubs in the last 5 years alone.

4–7x

Heatwave Days Projected by 2050

Major Indian cities face 4 to 7 times more severe heatwave cycles, accelerating membrane embrittlement.

Industry Practice Trap

Why 93% of Waterproofing Fails in Pre-Tender

What comes first: designing a project-specific engineered waterproofing specification, or directly floating a vague BOQ enquiry?

The "Cheap" Solution Trap

Clients seek offers from multiple product suppliers or applicators who propose vastly differing chemistries at wildly varying rates. Confused clients award to an insufficiently designed "cheap" bid.

"We want to eat mangoes, but evaluate oranges and then buy a banana."

Predefined Budgets & Single-Line BOQs

Budgets get fixed before assessing soil water tables and microclimate risks. Contracts are awarded with ambiguous single-line BOQs, inevitably causing extra items, spec deviations, and contractor disputes.
