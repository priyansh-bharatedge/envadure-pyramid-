Fundamental Failure Vectors

From the Analysis its Clear that Buildings Leak due to 5 fundamental reasons :

  1. Missed out areas of a structure that haven’t been waterproofed.
  2. Wrong Systems Selection or Deficient Specifications
  3. Application Errors
  4. Damage to the installed waterproofing
  5. Poor product quality
Points 1 & 2 - Account for Nearly 45% of Failures, Remaining 3 Account for 55% cases
Specification Breakdown

Wrong Systems Selection or Deficient Specifications

  • Lack of subject matter expertise among those drafting BOQ specifications.
  • Copy-paste approach: same BOQ used across multiple projects without evaluating project-specific requirements.
  • No thought of local climate, geotechnical/ geographic factors, building use, Construction methodology, buildability, maintenance
  • Insufficiently drafted BOQ specifications; unclear system build-up and protection layers.
  • Mixed multi-brand specs causing confusion on specification intent.
  • Single-product manufacturer-led monopolistic specification.
Vector 01

Missed out areas of a structure that haven’t been waterproofed

Why this happens and resultant problems :

  • Only standard surfaces ( like roofs , toilets , tanks, basement) considered while drafting BOQ – No in-depth study of project’s drawings to identify vulnerable that need waterproofing
  • Post award , Cost increases due to extra items getting executed at much higher prices – Typically what costs Re. 1/- during initial design phase estimation – can cost Rs. 2/- if identified later during execution and can cost upto Rs. 10/- as repair costs post hand-over.
  • Missed BOQ items or lack of scope clarity can result in potential disputes between Client , contractors, PMC
  • Developer’s brand image adversely impacted due – customer’s carry perception that critical areas seem omitted

EnvaDure impact -

  • Detailed engineering study of architectural and structural drawings , soil investigation report , geographical factors, planned usage , construction technique, future maintenance factors etc for holistic risk assessment to identify all locations that need waterproofing.
  • Aligned BOQ item list arrived at post detailed study, minimises risk of extra items and missed scope.
  • Architectural drawings marked up with locations that need waterproofing for easy correlation with BOQ items.
Vector 02

Wrong Systems Selection / Incorrect Specifications

Why this happens and resultant problems :

  • Lack of subject matter expertise with BOQ drafting team makers.
  • Adopting a copy paste approach of using one project’s BOQ across multiple projects without evaluating each individual project’s requirements.
  • Insufficiently drafted BOQ specifications that don’t provide clarity to bidders or project management teams – At times BOQ has a mix of multi brand based specs that creates confusion as specification intent isn’t clear.
  • Product manufacturer led BOQ can favour a specific brand – depriving client of a fair bidding process for best value option.

EnvaDure impact -

  • An independent brand agnostic neutral approach only driven by the project’s requirements.
  • A scientific approach towards fit for purpose based specifications design based on Indian and International standards and best practices.
  • Value engineered , Durability and Sustainability driven thought process.
  • Project biased and not product biased philosophy at the core.
  • No commercial involvement in entire process of bidding.
Vector 03

Erroneous Application done on site

Why this happens and resultant problems :

  • Untrained workmen , Insufficient supervision – outcome of selecting a waterproofing contractor only basis L1 pricing without a proper PQ process.
  • Project execution teams not knowing what to check , how to check and when to check during application
  • Specifications unclear about dealing with complex locations such as penetrations , interfaces , profile etc
  • Lack of clarity in method statements in dealing with complex site conditions.
  • No project specific customised cross section drawings available to enable waterproofing contractor and project team to understand intricacy of the waterproofing system installation & build-up at critical points.
  • Project teams and applicators unaware about material storage and material handling norms.

EnvaDure impact -

  • Project specific PQ criteria enabling client to select Waterproofing contractors who have capability to execute.
  • Detailed cross section drawings for all surfaces / BOQ items and step-wise detailed method statements.
  • Clearly defined Substrate parameters and ambient factors influencing application.
  • Clearly defined protocol for on-site tests to be performed.
  • Easy to implement QAQC check lists for monitoring the work being done by Waterproofing Contractor.
Vector 04

Damage to the waterproofing by other agencies

Why this happens and resultant problems :

  • Due to lack of proper activity sequence clarity in the specifications /method statements & unclear HOTO process between agencies.
  • Lack of clarity in specifications about how to protect, when to protect and who will do the protection to completed waterproofing.

EnvaDure impact -

  • Clearly defined scope matrix on technical parameters pertaining to HOTO between Waterproofing Contractor and Main Civil Contractor
  • Detailed method statements that defined sequential activity with clarity of time intervals involved in succeeding activities.
Vector 05

Due to Poor product quality

Why this happens and resultant problems :

  • Lack of Manufacturers Pre-qualification criteria.
  • Lack of knowledge in project team about what to check during product approval process, and what to test with testing frequency for product quality.

EnvaDure impact -

  • A neutral approach towards defining project specific PQ criteria enabling client to select Manufacturers/brands who can supply products meeting specification designs.
  • Clearly defined performance parameters for product approval
  • Clearly defined product testing protocols through 3rd party labs.
